TIP 01

🎯 Improve Your Aim

Practice your aim regularly in Training Mode. Focus on headshots and learn to control your weapon recoil.

TIP 02

🔫 Control Recoil

Every weapon has different recoil. Practice pulling your aim down while firing to keep your shots accurate.

TIP 03

👂 Use Sound

Footsteps and gun sounds can reveal enemy positions. Use headphones and pay attention to the direction of sounds.

TIP 04

🧱 Use Cover

Avoid standing in open areas during fights. Trees, rocks, walls and buildings can give you important cover.

TIP 05

🗺️ Learn the Map

Learn popular landing spots, compounds and rotations. Good map knowledge can help you choose better positions.

TIP 06

⚡ Improve Movement

Keep moving during fights and avoid predictable movement. Practice strafing, crouching and quick peeking.

TIP 07

🧠 Play Smart

Don't rush every enemy. Think before attacking and choose fights that give you a good advantage.

TIP 08

🎒 Manage Loot

Carry useful ammo, healing items and throwables. Avoid filling your bag with unnecessary items.

TIP 09

🔥 Use Grenades

Frag grenades can force enemies out of cover, while smoke grenades can help you rotate or revive teammates.

TIP 10

🏆 Stay Calm

Don't panic during close fights. Stay focused, keep your crosshair controlled and make smart decisions.

💡 PRO TIP

Don't change your sensitivity every day. Choose a comfortable sensitivity and practice with it consistently so your muscle memory can improve.
